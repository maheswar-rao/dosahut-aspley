any social post.

1. **Scratch. Sip. Save.** — playful, three beats, works at a glance from a table.
2. **Join the VIP Club. Win before you even order.** — leads with the reward.
3. **Free to join. Instant gift. One scan.** — most literal; best if the poster

Recommendation: **option 1** as the headline with option 3 as the sub-line —
the pun earns attention, the sub-line does the explaining.

## Layout notes for whoever sets this

- Page is 1123 × 1587 px authored at 96 px/inch = exactly 297 × 420 mm. The
  height is fixed, not auto — both artboards were measured and fit inside it
  (direction A leaves 99 px slack, B leaves 68 px), with a ~48 px bottom margin
  below the last line.
- QR no smaller than **60 × 60 mm** — an A3 poster on a wall gets scanned from
  two metres away, and phone cameras need the target size. Direction A uses
  66 mm, direction B uses 87 mm; both sit on a plain white plate with a quiet
  zone and nothing behind them.
- Keep the QR on a plain cream field with quiet space around it. Do not drop it
  on a photo or a gradient; the scan reliability falls off sharply.
- Brand colours already in use on the site: maroon `#570B0B`, orange `#F15A27`,
  cream `#FFF7F4`.
- Fonts on the site: Cormorant Garamond (display), Oswald (headings), Nunito
  (body). Check licensing before sending to a commercial printer.
- Bleed 3 mm, 300 dpi, CMYK for print.
